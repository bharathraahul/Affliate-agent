# Project Idea — Autonomous Companies / Personal Shopper Agent

Reference for the Affiliate-agent hackathon project. See also [hackathon.md](./hackathon.md) and [sponsor-tracks.md](./sponsor-tracks.md).

## Concept

"Autonomous companies" — an agent-run business that generates and monetizes leads with minimal human involvement.

Example vertical: **personal shopper agent**.

### Flow
1. **Generate ads** to attract leads.
   - Option A: generic ads ("a personal shopper that talks with you").
   - Option B: targeted ads ("buy this car").
2. **Lead engages in conversation** with the agent.
3. **Agent understands the lead's needs** through dialogue.
4. **Monetize the lead:**
   - Insert affiliate links matching their needs, OR
   - Contact businesses to sell them the qualified lead.

### Open questions
- Whether to build a website / landing page to look more professional.
- Generic vs. targeted ads (or both).

## Goals & constraints
- Team of 3.
- Limited time remaining.
- Want the product to **keep running after the hackathon** (not just a demo) — favors building a real, deployable end-to-end system.
- Must use **at least 3 of the 9 sponsor tracks** (see [sponsor-tracks.md](./sponsor-tracks.md)).
